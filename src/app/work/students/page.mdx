## Overview

Students of Strength is a US-based nonprofit that pairs mentors with students from under-resourced communities. Their hybrid tutoring model was scaling quickly, but mentors struggled to find the right learners and administrators lacked real-time visibility into program outcomes.

We partnered with the founding team to reimagine the entire experience—from research and service design to a new digital platform. The vision: an adaptive environment where mentors, students, and staff can collaborate effortlessly, backed by data-informed insights instead of manual spreadsheets.

Together we launched a unified web and mobile platform, rebuilt the tutor workspace, and layered in AI-assisted matching so every learner is connected to meaningful support within minutes.

## Crafting a learner-first journey

Through interviews and journey mapping, we uncovered friction across onboarding, scheduling, and progress tracking. Early prototypes prioritized clarity: mentors now see a real-time feed of suggested matches, students can review upcoming sessions from any device, and staff can orchestrate support in a single admin view.

## An AI layer with a human feel

We implemented an intelligence layer that considers academic goals, availability, session history, and mentor expertise to recommend matches within minutes. Administrators stay in control thanks to transparent signals that explain why each pairing appears.

## A resilient platform ready to scale

Design and engineering moved in lockstep to ship a modular component library, accessible UI foundations, and a cloud-native architecture. Automated dashboards now surface program impact, helping Students of Strength demonstrate outcomes to funders and expand into new districts.
