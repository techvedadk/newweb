## Overview

Overlapp set out to make planning and sharing experiences truly social. Traditional event apps felt transactional, while generic feeds lacked context. The founders asked us to articulate a product that balances curation with spontaneity—and to build the underlying systems that make it scalable.

Partnering from the earliest discovery sprints, we defined the brand language, product architecture, and AI roadmap that power the Overlapp experience today.

## Designing a living social identity

We co-created the Overlapp brand—defining tone, motion, and photography guidelines that feel at home across product UI, marketing, and community storytelling. A flexible component system keeps the experience consistent as the team introduces new events and collaborators.

## Intelligence that curates the vibe

An AI layer ingests interests, social signals, and availability to surface the right plans for each member. Hosts receive content prompts and scheduling nudges, while members get recommendations that feel personal instead of generic.

## Tooling built for a nimble team

Launching in a new city now takes days rather than months. We delivered editorial tooling, analytics dashboards, and a cross-platform codebase that shares 80% of its logic between mobile and web—keeping the small Overlapp team focused on community.
