Minlandsby enables citizens, civic groups, and local governments to collaborate through a shared digital space. Their legacy app struggled with engagement, accessibility, and moderation, making it difficult to sustain trust between stakeholders.

We were invited to chart a new course—crafting a people-first experience that scales to hundreds of communities while keeping the conversation respectful and relevant.

## Designing for civic trust

Interviews with residents, volunteers, and council staff revealed the need for transparency and easy contribution. We redesigned navigation around community goals—neighbourhood updates, volunteer efforts, local commerce—so everyone immediately understands how to participate.

## Intelligence that supports moderators

An AI assistant now highlights trending topics, spots potential misinformation, and suggests responses while keeping humans firmly in control. Moderators receive clear context for every recommendation, making it easier to keep communities safe and constructive.

## A platform ready for every town

We rebuilt the mobile app in Flutter with shared UI primitives, introduced automated deployment pipelines, and created an admin console for municipalities. The result is a flexible platform that helps Minlandsby expand into new regions without losing the local touch.
